# Decision 0003 - Visual identity and UX direction (owner directive)

**Date:** 2026-08-23
**Source:** Owner instruction during planning sprint P1
**Supersedes:** the glass-first framing in blueprint s13 and any glass-centric wording inherited into `planning-artifacts/prd.md`

## Owner directives

1. **Name must be music-first.** Not about glass or materials - it should evoke sound, rhythm, melody, listening. Dedicated naming study commissioned: `docs/research/naming-study.md`.
2. **Design language: first-class, aesthetic, buttery smooth.** Quality bar benchmarked against Apple/iOS-grade polish: fluid motion, restraint, perfect spacing/typography, no jank.
3. **Foundation: Material 3 Expressive** (latest M3 expressive components/motion), customized with our own tokens - not stock Material look, not a transparency gimmick showcase.
4. **Keep SuvMusic as experience inspiration only**: its player transitions, artwork presentation, and performance work remain study targets (blueprint s13 study list stands); its visuals/branding are not copied.
5. Artwork remains central to the experience (color extraction, large-format presentation), expressed through smooth surfaces and motion rather than heavy frosted-glass effects.

## Consequences

- [CU] `bmad-ux` session must be briefed with THIS direction (M3 Expressive + iOS-grade smoothness + artwork-driven color), not blueprint s13's liquid-glass system text.
- PRD provisional items touching visual identity (name Vitro, liquid-glass wording) require a small PRD amendment once the owner locks the name from the naming study.
- Blueprint s13 stays as historical research input; this file overrides it for all downstream artifacts.
