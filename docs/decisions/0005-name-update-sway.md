# Decision 0005 - Name update: Sway

**Date:** 2026-08-23
**Decided by:** Owner (Hemant), direct choice
**Supersedes:** Decision 0004 ("Medley") - kept as history only

## Decision

The product and app are named **Sway**.

- Rationale (owner): simple and catchy; to sway is to move with the rhythm - the exact feeling of buttery-smooth playback and fluid transitions.
- Tagline: "Your music, in flow."
- Naming history: glass-themed names rejected (0003), classical terms rejected after study (`docs/research/naming-study.md`), "Medley" briefly locked then replaced by owner preference.

## Consequences

- Application label: Sway. Working package id until release check: `com.sway.music`.
- PRD + UX spec patched to Sway.
- REMAINING before public release: trademark + Play Store collision verification for "Sway". Known item to check: Microsoft's retired Office Sway (category-distant, likely lapsed, but must be verified).
